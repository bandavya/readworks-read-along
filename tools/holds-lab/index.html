    Pronunciation words this check can miss

    Words such as read, lead, wind, tear, bow, live, minute, wound, close, record can sound different while their spelling stays the same. A text comparison can miss a wrong pronunciation. A person can listen, or a confirmed Polly error can receive a context-specific <w role="amazon:..."> rule. Polly w-tag guide. A listed word is only a candidate.

    Who could own a hold?

    Recommended: ReadWorks names a content editor for word and pronunciation decisions; engineering handles gate configuration and broken runs. Alternatives are an audio producer, or the narrator followed by an editor. No owner has been assigned.
