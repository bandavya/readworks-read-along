# Sound Along: evaluation plan

Draft of November 4, 2026, for team and ReadWorks review. Nothing here has been run.
Numbers marked "proposed" are starting points to agree, not standards. Decision numbers
(D1, D2, ...) match `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md`.

Inputs: `Sound_Along_AWS_Compatible_Technical_Design.docx` (sections 6.1, 12, 15),
`Sound Along — TTS Validation Design Doc.docx` (acceptance evaluation, scorecard),
`docs/SPEC.md` (current state and test status).

---

## 1. Purpose

This evaluation answers one question: **is this voice, pipeline and reader good enough to move
to a pilot?** It is a one-off study, repeated when the voice, engine or reader changes.

It is not the per-passage check that runs on every recording. That gate is described in the
validation doc and built in `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md` (Phase 1). The two are linked: this study
decides the thresholds the gate will use (timing tolerance, rating bar, chosen voice).

**Decision this feeds (to confirm):** adopt Polly neural audio for the pilot, and pick the
default voice. Outcomes are Go, Go with conditions, or Not yet (section 8).

## 2. Scope

In scope

- English passages generated by this pipeline, played in the reader app.
- Comparison against the audio ReadWorks already has.
- One volunteer-style recording, to show the second audio source works (not a volunteer program).

Out of scope

- Children as participants. Any child sessions go through ReadWorks' own process.
- The full catalog, Spanish, teacher features, and the volunteer track beyond one recording.
- Certifying accessibility. A script or this team cannot certify it (section 6.6).

## 3. What is evaluated

| ID | Workstream | Question |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Voice quality | Does it sound natural, well paced and right for the age? |
| V2 | Pronunciation | Are names, numbers and vocabulary said correctly? |
| V3 | Fidelity | Does the audio say the words in the text, with none skipped, swapped or added? |
| V4 | Highlight timing | Does the highlighted word match the word being spoken? |
| V5 | Reader behaviour | Do the design's tests T01 to T14 pass in the reader? |
| V6 | Accessibility | Can the core controls be used by keyboard and screen reader? |
| V7 | Cost | What does a passage and the catalog cost, with the corrected cost metric? |
| V8 | Operation and safety | Does it fail visibly, retry, stay private, and handle data properly? |

## 6. Methods

### 6.1 V1 Voice quality: blind listening test

- **Panel:** 8 to 12 adults (D8). This is a small panel, so results are indicative, not
  statistically conclusive. Report spread, not only averages.
- **Clips:** the same passage text in every voice, excerpted to 60 to 90 seconds, each clip
  given a random code. Order is randomised per rater and per passage. The code-to-voice key is
  held apart from raters.
- **Per clip, the rater records:**

| Field | Scale |
|---|---|
| Naturalness | 1 to 5 |
| Pacing at normal speed | 1 to 5 |
| Pacing at 0.75x acceptable? | yes / no |
| Age fit for the grade | 1 to 5 |
| Words said wrongly | free text (feeds V2) |
| Overall preference within the passage | rank |

- **Load:** 7 passages x 3 to 4 clips x about 45 seconds is roughly 20 minutes of listening, plus
  rating. Plan about 40 minutes per rater (estimate).
- **Analysis:** mean and distribution per voice; the share of raters preferring each voice over the
  baseline; per-passage minimums; and a note on any split between raters.
- **Briefing:** raters are told what to judge and that voices are unlabelled. They are not told which
  clip is human or generated.

### 6.2 V2 Pronunciation

Raters list wrong words in the form above. A reviewer checks every name, number and
vocabulary word in the evaluated passages. Each miss goes into the Polly lexicon so it is fixed
catalog-wide, or is logged as an exception with a reason. Nothing is only noted per passage.

### 6.3 V3 Fidelity

- Run L3 on every evaluated passage: transcribe, align to the spoken script, classify differences.
- **Planted-error test:** synthesize the passage with one word changed and check it against the
  original script. The check must flag the change. A check that cannot detect a planted error does not count.
- Report unexplained differences per passage after classification.
- A clean transcript shows the words are present. It does not show how they sounded, which is V1 and V2.

### 6.4 V4 Highlight timing

Two kinds of evidence, because two machines agreeing is not the same as being right.

| Evidence | How |
|---|---|
| Machine agreement | Compare speech-mark word times with Transcribe word times (L5): median, p90, share beyond 150 ms and 300 ms |
| Gold set | A team member hand-marks word onsets on 1 to 2 passages in Audacity (about an hour each); report our timing against those marks |
| Listener judgement | Raters watch the highlight at 1x and 0.75x and mark words that feel early or late |

Proposed bar (D1): p90 onset error at most 150 ms against the gold set, and under 1% of words
beyond 300 ms. A "none beyond 300 ms" rule was dropped because the comparison tool has its own
noise. Words the transcriber did not recognise are excluded from the machine comparison and
counted separately.

### 6.6 V6 Accessibility

Name the standard (D7). WCAG 2.2 AA is the usual choice. Results are findings, not a
certification: full validation needs testing with assistive technology and expert review.

Tester checklist (one person with a screen reader, plus a real iPad or phone):

- Keyboard only: reach the library, open a passage, play, pause, change speed, switch voice, use word help, tap a word.
- Focus is always visible and the order makes sense.
- Text enlarges to 200% without losing content or controls.
- Contrast of text, highlight and controls.
- Screen reader (VoiceOver or NVDA): controls are named, state changes are announced, the passage can be read.
- Touch targets are usable on a tablet.
- Motion respects the reduced-motion setting.

An automated scan can supplement this. It does not replace it.

### 6.7 V7 Cost

Use the corrected cost metric (L2) and Cost Explorer. Verified rates (AWS Polly page):
standard $4, neural $16, generative $30 per million characters. Polly bills **both** the audio and the
speech-marks request. Catalog size is the design doc's 20 million characters (6,893 passages).

| Setup, per voice, whole catalog | Calculation | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Design doc figure (standard, audio only) | 20M x $4/M | $80 |
| Neural, audio only | 20M x $16/M | $320 |
| Neural with speech marks (what we run) | 20M x 2 x $16/M | $640 |
| Generative, audio only | 20M x $30/M | $600 (marks pricing not verified) |
| Validation (Transcribe, 100% of passages) | 22,000 min x $0.006 | about $130 (confirm us-west-2 rate) |

These exclude the free tier, retries, regeneration and reviewer time. Each added voice adds its
Polly cost and its validation cost.

Report: measured characters, cost per passage, and the catalog projection against the budget in D10.

### 6.8 V8 Operation and safety

- Failure visible and retryable, duplicate skipped, private storage: re-run T10, T11, T13 on the final build.
- High-severity findings from the AWS MCP review (open public site, unauthenticated routes, deploys
  without an approval step) are fixed or explicitly accepted in writing before any real student use.
- Raters are adults; store only a rater code, not names, with ratings. Ratings are kept in a
  private location and not committed to a repository.
- Generated audio and definitions are drafts until a person approves them.

## 7. Pass criteria (proposed)

| Workstream | Proposed bar | Agreed by |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Naturalness mean at least 4 of 5; no passage mean below 3; preference share versus baseline reported and discussed | ReadWorks |
| V2 | Every miss fixed in the lexicon or logged as an exception; no unresolved miss on a name or number | ReadWorks |
| V3 | Planted error detected; no unexplained content-word difference left after classification | Team |
| V4 | p90 at most 150 ms against the gold set; under 1% beyond 300 ms; fewer than 5% of words flagged early or late by listeners (placeholder) | ReadWorks (D1) |
| V5 | All tests pass, or each gap accepted in writing | Team |
| V6 | No blocking issue for the core controls by keyboard or screen reader; other issues listed with owners | ReadWorks (D7) |
| V7 | Projected catalog cost within the budget in D10 | ReadWorks |
| V8 | High-severity findings closed or accepted | Team |

## 8. Decision rule

- **Go:** V1 to V6 meet their bars, V7 is within budget, V8 items are closed.
- **Go with conditions:** a short, named list of gaps with owners and dates (for example a lexicon fix, a tolerance to revisit).
- **Not yet:** state what changes (different voice or engine, speech rate, lexicon, timing method) and re-run
  only the affected workstreams.

What the study sets for the pipeline gate: the timing tolerance, the rating bar, the chosen
voice, and the words-per-minute band per grade measured on the passages.
